## 1. The 3 Core Viral Gaming Archetypes

### A. The Impossible Boss Encounter (Scale Contrast)
* **Visual Math**: Boss occupies **65%** of the frame on the right side; small player character occupies **20%** on the bottom-left. The 3:1 scale difference signals extreme vulnerability.
* **Lighting**: Boss silhouette glows with a furious colored rim light (vermilion red or radioactive violet) contrasting with cold neutral background.
* **Text Hook**: `IMPOSSIBLE`, `DO NOT FIGHT`, `1% SURVIVAL`.

### B. The 1-in-a-Million Loot / Glitch Event (Dopamine Discovery)
* **Visual Math**: Ultra-rare item or glitched entity isolated in the center-third with radiant golden or celestial turquoise god-rays beaming outward.
* **Player Reaction / Anchor**: Hand reaching into the light or character pointing in disbelief.
* **Text Hook**: `1 IN 10,000,000`, `IT HAPPENED`, `BANNED ITEM`.

### C. The Hardcore Survival Climax ("Day 100" / "Last Stand")
* **Visual Math**: Burning fortress or ruined stronghold in background; heavily armored survivor with 1% HP bar and shattered shield in foreground.
* **Atmosphere**: Sparks, ember particles, rain, cracked visor/lens vignette.
* **Text Hook**: `DAY 100`, `HE LOST EVERYTHING`, `NEVER AGAIN`.
