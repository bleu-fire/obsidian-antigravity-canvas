## 1. The Universal Cognitive Framework (Pre-Attentive Processing)

Viewers evaluate a thumbnail on mobile within **100 to 150 milliseconds**. At that speed, cognitive comprehension has not triggered—the visual operates entirely on primal visual processing:

1. **The Curiosity Gap Rule**:
   - **Never duplicate the video title.** If the title is *"How the Roman Empire Actually Fell"*, the thumbnail text is *"THE BETRAYAL"*, not the title.
   - The image shows a tension or unresolved conflict that can only be understood by clicking.
2. **The 3-Element Ceiling**:
   - Maximum 3 items total: **[1 Anchor Subject] + [1 Context Cue / Background] + [1 Typographic Hook]**.
   - If an element does not drive the curiosity gap, remove it.
3. **The Squint Test (Mobile 120×67px)**:
   - Scale down to 10% zoom. If the focal point and silhouette merge into grey mush, the contrast ratio has failed.
4. **The Safe Zone**:
   - The bottom-right 20% area is covered by the YouTube duration timestamp. Keep all text and focal objects in the upper-left, center, or left-third.

## 2. Six Genre Visual Playbooks

### A. Documentary (Investigation & Revelations)
- **Examples**: Johnny Harris, Lemmino, Vox, Fern.
- **Psychological Driver**: Uncovering classified or hidden systemic truths.
- **Color Palette**: Deep archival navy (`#0B132B`), aged document cream, single selective fluorescent highlighter (amber or caution yellow).
- **Lighting & Texture**: Halftone risograph textures, subtle topographical grid lines, volumetric document dust, chiaroscuro spotlight.
- **Subject Formulation**: Single declassified file, satellite coordinate mark, macro close-up of a critical physical evidence piece.
- **Copy Formula**: Maximum 2 to 3 words: `THE COVERUP`, `HOW THEY HID IT`, `THE LEAK`.

### B. History (Epic Scale & Inevitable Collapse)
- **Examples**: Dan Carlin, Kings and Generals, Fall of Civilizations.
- **Psychological Driver**: Magnitude, hubris, irreversible turning points.
- **Color Palette**: Weathered stone basalt, blood vermilion, oxidized bronze, warm antique gold.
- **Lighting & Texture**: Low-angle dramatic torchlight or sun cutting through smoke, chipped marble fractures, heavy atmospheric haze.
- **Subject Formulation**: Weathered sculptural portrait with missing eye/fracture, monumental battlefield map with red pincer arrows, royal seal cracked in half.
- **Copy Formula**: `THE LAST DAY`, `WHY IT FELL`, `POINT OF NO RETURN`.

### C. Gaming (Adrenaline, Mastery & Impossible Feats)
- **Examples**: High-level speedruns, esports breakdowns, survival challenges.
- **Psychological Driver**: High stakes, extreme difficulty, elite game knowledge.
- **Color Palette**: High-saturation electric cyan (`#00F0FF`), acid lime, deep carbon fiber black.
- **Lighting & Texture**: Intense back rim lighting (halo effect), motion speed lines, sharp polygon edges, high-contrast glow.
- **Subject Formulation**: In-game boss silhouette dwarfing the player, countdown timer at 00:01, impossible item stat comparison.
- **Copy Formula**: `1 IN 1,000,000`, `IMPOSSIBLE`, `DO NOT DIE`.

### D. Dramatic Storytelling & Cinematic Biographies
- **Examples**: MagnatesMedia, Moon, Aperture.
- **Psychological Driver**: The dramatic rise and catastrophic fall of powerful human figures.
- **Color Palette**: High-contrast noir, deep charcoal, single warm tungsten spotlight.
- **Lighting & Texture**: Severe Rembrandt split lighting (one side illuminated, other in pitch darkness), subtle grain.
- **Subject Formulation**: Severe human profile staring away from the camera, shattered glass reflection, split portrait (glory vs ruin).
- **Copy Formula**: `HE LOST EVERYTHING`, `THE GREED`, `THE COLLAPSE`.

### E. Horror & Psychological Thrillers
- **Examples**: Local58, Wendigoon, Nexpo, Analog Horror.
- **Psychological Driver**: The uncanny, dread of the unseen, cognitive dissonance.
- **Color Palette**: Sickly VHS tape green, pitch black void (`#030508`), washed-out flashlight bone-white.
- **Lighting & Texture**: Extreme narrow beam flashlight with harsh falloff, analog VHS scan lines, sensor noise, silhouette standing slightly off-center in the background.
- **Subject Formulation**: Open doorway into pure darkness, blurred figure looking directly at the camera, distorted audio waveform.
- **Copy Formula**: `NEVER GO HERE`, `IT FOUND US`, `DO NOT LOOK`.

### F. Political Thrillers & Geopolitical Conflict
- **Examples**: RealLifeLore, CaspianReport, TLDR News.
- **Psychological Driver**: Power struggles, borders, sovereign consequences.
- **Color Palette**: Strategic slate grey, tactical military olive, stark emergency red.
- **Lighting & Texture**: Cold overhead briefing room fluorescent, radar sweep overlays, border wire textures.
- **Subject Formulation**: Two state leaders facing opposite directions, geopolitical choke point map (Strait of Malacca / Suez), nuclear silo hatch silhouette.
- **Copy Formula**: `WAR IN 48 HOURS`, `THE RED LINE`, `WHO CONTROLS IT`.

## 3. Obsidian Canvas Spatial Architecture

When applying this skill to Obsidian Canvas:

```text
Coordinates Formula:
Origin Concept Node: (x, y)
├─ Genre Strategic Brief Card:      (x, y + height + 100, width: 340, height: 220)
├─ 16:9 Image Asset (Antigravity):   (x + 380, y + height + 100, width: 480, height: 270)
└─ Editorial Hook & Wireframe Frame: (x + 900, y + height + 100, width: 620, height: 270)
```

- **Wireframe Group**: 16:9 ratio container (`width: 620, height: 270`).
- **Hook Headline Card**: Placed in top-left scan zone (`width: 280, height: 180`).
- **Context Badge Card**: Placed in right quadrant (`width: 260, height: 180`).
- **Connectors**: Labeled with genre progression (`"Strategic Angle"`, `"Visual Production"`).
