## Part 1: Universal Thumbnail Rules & Cognitive Math

Viewers evaluate a thumbnail in **100 to 150 milliseconds** on mobile feeds. Cognitive comprehension has not yet triggered; the visual operates entirely on primal visual processing.

1. **The 1-Second Recognition Rule**: The core subject, emotional state, and narrative tension must be understood within 1000ms at any scale.
2. **The Mobile Squint Test (120x67px)**: Scale the thumbnail down to 10% size. If the focal silhouette merges into grey background noise, the contrast ratio and edge separation have failed.
3. **The Safe Zone Protection Area**: The bottom-right 20% of every 16:9 thumbnail is obscured by the platform duration badge (`14:28`). Never place critical focal subjects, facial expressions, or typography in this quadrant.
4. **Detail Density Hierarchy**: Never distribute detail evenly across the canvas. Detail must be concentrated at 90% density on the focal anchor (eyes, weapon edge, logo), dropping to 30% in the midground, and 5% (soft atmospheric blur) in the background.

## Part 2: The Adaptive Style Engine & Hybridization Rules

The engine analyzes content along 4 vectors:
- **Title & Hook**: Tone, urgency, topic domain.
- **Target Audience**: Mainstream consumers, tech professionals, gamers, or students.
- **Emotional Vector**: Curiosity, adrenaline, awe, dread, authority, or inspiration.
- **Visual Subject**: Human face, hardware product, infographic, or dramatic scene.

### Controlled Hybridization (The 70/30 Rule)
When combining styles, maintain a strict hierarchy to prevent aesthetic chaos:
* **70%–80% Dominant Style**: Sets composition, lighting direction, and overall color gamut.
* **20%–30% Secondary Style**: Injects accent effects, material textures, or typographic treatment.

**Approved Hybrid Pairings**:
* *Cinematic + Documentary*: Deep chiaroscuro lighting on authentic archival subject matter.
* *Gaming + 3D Futuristic*: High-octane esports action staged with Octane 3D PBR materials.
* *Business + Luxury*: Data-driven executive charts rendered with gold/titanium accents.
* *News + Photorealistic*: Urgent headline framing using photojournalistic street photography optics.
* *AI/Tech + Futuristic*: Clean technical UI elements with volumetric neon telemetry.
* *Educational + Minimal*: Complex scientific concepts simplified into bold geometric visual metaphors.

### Style 1: Cinematic / Movie

1. **Visual Identity**: High-budget film still, narrative gravitas, epic theatrical atmosphere. Conveys consequential, high-stakes storytelling.
2. **Composition**: Asymmetrical 60/40 rule. Dominant subject placed along left or right vertical third. Strong leading diagonal lines created by architecture, weapons, or headlights.
3. **Subject Treatment**: Medium shot or close profile. Dynamic action pose or contemplative gaze directed across the frame into negative space. Razor-sharp silhouette edge separation.
4. **Lighting**: Low-key chiaroscuro. 45-degree hard key light, subtle neutral fill (8:1 contrast ratio), intense volumetric rim light carving subject contours out of pitch darkness.
5. **Color System**: Teal and Orange Hollywood contrast, or monochromatic charcoal accented by intense tungsten amber. Maximum 3 dominant colors.
6. **Background**: Cinematic environment with atmospheric perspective (smoke, drifting embers, rain, or heat distortion). Background depth of field blurred to f/1.4.
7. **Typography**: Trajan or bold cinematic serif. Wide letter-spacing (0.15em). Placed in upper-third negative space. Maximum 3 words.
8. **Thumbnail Readability**: High-contrast silhouette readable at 10% zoom. Subject separation maintained via specular rim highlight.
9. **Depth & Separation**: 3 distinct layers (blurred foreground particle, razor-sharp hero subject, atmospheric deep background).
10. **Detail Density**: 90% detail on hero facial expression and costume texture; 10% on soft background falloff.
11. **Camera & Lens**: 50mm or 85mm Anamorphic lens, f/1.4 aperture, subtle horizontal streak flares, 2.39:1 widescreen frame sense inside 16:9.
12. **Effects**: Volumetric light shafts, micro dust motes, realistic lens dirt. FORBIDDEN: Cartoon glows, saturated neon stickers, flat graphics.
13. **Quality Control Negative**: `flat lighting, oversaturated colors, cheap neon, centered boring pose, cluttered background, blurry subject, plastic skin`.

### Style 2: YouTube Viral

1. **Visual Identity**: Maximum ocular shock, extreme dopamine hook, pre-attentive curiosity gap. Built for 30M+ view mainstream entertainment.
2. **Composition**: Rule of thirds with exaggerated scale contrast (e.g. 3:1 scale ratio between giant threat and tiny underdog protagonist).
3. **Subject Treatment**: Extreme expressive close-up or mid-action jump. Wide eyes looking directly into the camera lens. Razor-sharp edge isolation.
4. **Lighting**: Bright commercial three-point lighting supplemented by an intense electric cyan or neon yellow rim halo (50% backlight overdrive).
5. **Color System**: Triadic high-saturation palette (Electric Yellow `#FFD700`, Vivid Red `#FF0033`, Pure Cyan `#00F0FF`) against clean dark or high-key backing.
6. **Background**: Simplified contextual setting (burning room, bank vault, ocean trench). Minimal clutter; every background element reinforces the crisis.
7. **Typography**: Ultra-bold sans-serif (Impact, Montserrat Black). Heavy black outline (8px) + dropped shadow. Maximum 2 to 3 words: "HE DIED", "1 IN 1,000,000".
8. **Thumbnail Readability**: Instant 100ms comprehension at 120px mobile width. The facial expression and central dilemma are unmistakable.
9. **Depth & Separation**: Hard cutout separation between subject and background using an artificial 2px rim glow stroke or 100% backlit halo.
10. **Detail Density**: 100% focus on hyper-expressive eyes, mouth, and hero object. Background textures softened to avoid ocular distraction.
11. **Camera & Lens**: 24mm wide-angle lens close to subject creating dramatic dynamic perspective distortion.
12. **Effects**: Speed motion lines, comic-style impact bursts, glowing arrows, high-intensity rim rays. FORBIDDEN: Desaturated palettes, subtle artistic ambiguity.
13. **Quality Control Negative**: `subtle expression, washed out low contrast, complex unreadable background, small text, dull colors, boring neutral pose`.

10. **Detail Density**: Extreme micro-texture detail on watch bezels, leather grain, and brushed titanium; zero texture on background void.
