## 1. Material Specs for Macro Gaming Loot

* **Damascus / Folded Steel**: Rippling acid-etched woodgrain patterns with mirror-polished micro-beveled cutting edges.
* **Cybernetic Titanium**: Anisotropic brushed gunmetal texture, heat-tinted purple/blue exhaust nozzles, fiber-optic bundle wiring.
* **Smoked Optical Obsidian**: Translucent black volcanic glass with internal refractive flaws and deep internal gold luminescence.
* **Ancient Gold Filigree**: Chiseled relief inlays with weathered verdigris or bloodstone settings.
