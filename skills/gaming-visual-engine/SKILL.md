## 1. The Four Gaming Sub-Genres

### A. Grimdark Soulsborne (Elden Ring / Bloodborne / Dark Souls)
* **Emotional Hook**: Overwhelming colossal scale, existential struggle, ancient ruin.
* **Palette**: Weathered basalt grey, oxidized bronze, cold ash, single selective incandescent golden fire or blood-crimson embers.
* **Lighting & Shaders**: Heavy chiaroscuro, torchlight cutting through heavy catacomb fog, specular reflections on damp cobblestones and pitted iron plate armor.
* **Prompt Formula**:
  ```text
  Unreal Engine 5 AAA gaming keyart, 16:9 composition. Grimdark Soulsborne epic scene of [SUBJECT FROM CONTEXT].
  Scale: Colossal monumental architecture dwarfing the solitary armored protagonist.
  Lighting: Volumetric torchlight and glowing golden embers cutting through dense atmospheric fog, deep pitch shadows.
  Materiality: Weathered pitted plate armor with chipped heraldry, damp fractured stone, natural rust and blood patina.
  Optics & Render: Cinematic wide shot, 8k raytracing, Nanite geometric detail, Lumen global illumination, zero cartoon.
  --no flat vector, no blurry textures, no plastic armor, no clean modern graphics
  ```

---

### B. Cyberpunk Esports (Apex Legends / Cyberpunk 2077 / Valorant)
* **Emotional Hook**: Adrenaline, high velocity, cybernetic enhancements, mechanical precision.
* **Palette**: High-contrast carbon fiber black, electric cyan (`#00F0FF`), acid neon yellow, ultraviolet magenta.
* **Lighting & Shaders**: Intense back rim lighting (halo effect), holographic optical sights, dynamic motion blur on edges, reflective wet asphalt.
* **Prompt Formula**:
  ```text
  Dynamic AAA esports promotional keyart, 16:9 composition. High-octane cyberpunk action featuring [SUBJECT FROM CONTEXT].
  Lighting: Blinding neon cyan and magenta rim lights tracing matte carbon composite armor, holographic reticle glare.
  Detail: Intricate mechanical servo joints, heat dissipation vents glowing orange, micro-etched corporate sponsor typography.
  Atmosphere: Atmospheric rain spray, high-speed camera motion lines, high-contrast octane render, razor-sharp 8k fidelity.
  --no muddy colors, no washed out lighting, no low-poly, no cartoon face
  ```

---

### C. Mythic High-Fantasy RPG (World of Warcraft / Final Fantasy XIV / Baldur's Gate 3)
* **Emotional Hook**: Legendary loot, celestial sorcery, mythic creature encounters.
* **Palette**: Deep royal midnight sapphire, radiant arcane gold, prismatic crystal dispersion.
* **Lighting & Shaders**: Radiant emissive rune glows, volumetric spell casting particles, iridescence on dragon scales and damascus steel.
* **Prompt Formula**:
  ```text
  Cinematic fantasy RPG splash art, 16:9 widescreen composition of [SUBJECT FROM CONTEXT].
  Magic Effects: Intricate glowing arcane glyphs circling the weapon/caster, floating crystalline dust motes catching celestial moonlight.
  Materials: Filigree gold-inlaid ornate armor, etched Damascus steel blade, velvet cape catching backlighting.
  Style: Riot Games / Blizzard cinematic splash art quality, deep artistic lighting, painterly realism, pristine 8k render.
  --no amateurish 3D, no stiff poses, no low resolution, no deformed limbs
  ```

---

### D. Tactical Hardcore Shooter (Tarkov / Modern Warfare / STALKER)
* **Emotional Hook**: Lethal realism, claustrophobia, night-vision surveillance, survival grit.
* **Palette**: Tactical olive drab, matte charcoal, weathered coyote tan, monochrome night vision green/phosphor.
* **Lighting & Shaders**: Low-light high-ISO camera sensor grain, incandescent tactical flashlight with harsh hard-shadow falloff, hot muzzle brake smoke.
* **Prompt Formula**:
  ```text
  Photorealistic tactical military gaming still, 16:9 composition of [SUBJECT FROM CONTEXT].
  Gear: Realistic plate carrier with modular pouches, weathered Cerakote firearm with optical sight, night vision mount.
  Lighting: Tactical weapon light cutting through dust-filled interior, harsh high-contrast chiaroscuro, gritty realistic atmosphere.
  Render: Photorealistic game capture, 8k photographic plate, real ballistic physics details, zero fantasy elements.
  --no cartoon, no neon colors, no unrealistic clean gear, no anime tropes
  ```
